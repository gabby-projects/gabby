## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers help keep the project healthy and welcoming. Their responsibilities include:

- Reviewing pull requests and giving clear, constructive feedback.
- Triaging issues and helping contributors find the right project area or owner.
- Guiding architectural discussions and recording significant decisions in ADRs.
- Keeping contribution guidance, user documentation, and the roadmap accurate.
- Maintaining CI, release checks, repository settings, and package publishing configuration.
- Responding to vulnerability reports privately under the [security policy](SECURITY.md).
- Following the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and addressing concerns through its process.

The project maintainer and architect is responsible for final decisions on project direction and
accepted architecture. Maintainer status does not replace the review and checks required by the
repository's branch protection settings.

## Becoming a maintainer

Gabby welcomes contributors who want to take on a sustained role in the project. Maintainer
appointments are based on a contributor's ongoing contributions, review and collaboration history,
understanding of Gabby's architecture and responsibilities, and interest in helping care for the
project. The current maintainer discusses the scope and expectations with the contributor before
proposing the appointment.

Appointments are recorded by updating this file through a reviewed pull request. Organization
membership, repository access, and GitHub team assignments are handled separately by the appropriate
organization or repository administrators.
